# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you interact with our services, including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details.

**Technical Data:** We also collect IP address, device type, browser information, and session data for analytics and security purposes.

## 2. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

- SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions
- Secure payment gateways — no raw card data stored
- Regular security audits and system updates

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us process orders, improve our services, and provide relevant communication about your purchases.

**Order Processing:** Payment, shipping, and customer service.

**Communication:** Order updates and support, only with your consent.

## 4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to maintain your shopping cart, remember preferences, and analyse site traffic to improve your experience.

**Essential Cookies:** Required for core functionality including checkout and cart management.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights or opt out of marketing communications at any time — no questions asked.

## 6. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party providers such as payment processors and shipping partners solely to fulfil your orders. We do not sell your data to any third party.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated via email or a notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
